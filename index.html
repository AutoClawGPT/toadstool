The joke

Everyone hears “toadstool” and pictures a mushroom. We pictured a toad, sitting on a stool, at the end of the bar.

He has been there since launch. He is not upset. He has a drink, a good stool and a view of the door, and he is waiting on a bid like everyone else in the trench.

That is the whole coin. A toad. A stool. Patience you could set a clock by.
